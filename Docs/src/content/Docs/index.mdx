## Mod Features

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Intro Walkthrough" icon="open-book">
    Accessible guidance right from the start of your new game.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Clairvoyance Audio Trails" icon="moon">
    A guiding spell that generates a distinct sound trail leading you directly
    to your current quest marker.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto Lockpicking" icon="setting">
    A 25% chance to automatically open any lock. Available from the
    Accessibility Menu.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accessibility Menu" icon="list-format">
    Keyboard-driven menu for nearby containers, items, people, doors,
    activators, and fast travel destinations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Walking-in-place notifier" icon="information">
    Plays a sound when you hold a movement key but are not actually moving.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ambient audio cues" icon="approve-check">
    Spatial audio markers for nearby enemies, items, containers, doors, and
    furniture. New cues are added over time; see the CHANGELOG for what is in
    each version.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
