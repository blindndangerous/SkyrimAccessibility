## Mod Features

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Intro Walkthrough" icon="open-book">
		Accessible guidance right from the start of your new game.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Clairvoyance Audio Trails" icon="moon">
		A guiding spell that generates a distinct sound trail leading you directly
		to your current quest marker.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Auto Lockpicking" icon="setting">
		A 25% chance to automatically open any lock. Available exclusively via the
		Interact Menu.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Under Development" icon="warning">
		Sound notifications for walking in place, and spatial audio markers for
		items, NPCs, containers, and doors.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
